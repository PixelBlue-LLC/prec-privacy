Prec

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

The short version: Prec stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your personal information. Nothing leaves your phone.

1. Overview

Prec ("the App") is a preparation checklist application for iOS. This Privacy Policy describes how Prec handles your information. We take your privacy seriously — which is why we designed Prec to work entirely on-device, with no accounts, no servers, and no data collection.

2. Data We Collect

We collect no personal data.

Prec does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. This includes:

  • No name, email address, or contact information
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers
  • No usage analytics or behavioral data
  • No crash reports sent to external servers

3. How Your Data Is Stored

All data you create in Prec — your scenes, checklists, and items — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage framework (SwiftData). This data:

  • Never leaves your device through the App
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Is included in your standard iCloud or iTunes device backup, if you have backups enabled
  • Is permanently deleted when you delete the App

4. Third-Party Services

Prec does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no SDKs from Facebook, Google, or any other company embedded in the App.

5. iCloud Backup

Your device data may be included in iCloud backups as part of your normal iOS backup process. This is controlled by your iCloud settings, not by Prec. For information on how Apple handles your backup data, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

Prec does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because we collect no data at all, Prec is safe for users of all ages.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy — for example, if a future version of Prec adds cloud features or analytics — we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date. We will also notify users within the App when significant changes occur.
